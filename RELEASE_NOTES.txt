19th Jan 2026
=============

Better support for Irish Trigs:
Grid references are now shown in Irish grid format
The thumbnail map on the details pages defaults to OpenTopoMap
.
Dark mode is now available:
Click the button in the titlebar to switch between light, dark and auto modes
.
Active stations are now separately toggleable in the map and trigpoint list pages
User-added is no longer a category, and these are now listed under "Other" until they have been reviewed
.
The trigpoint list now defaults to a maximum distance of 200km, to improve performance
Ensure you push it back up to ∞ before downloading, if you want the whole UK dataset
.
Fixed the forum's Attachments tab. You can now upload photos / screenshots and include them in forum posts.
